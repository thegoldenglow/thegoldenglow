🤖 Telegram Bot Setup

Configure your Golden Glow Telegram bot for Netlify deployment

📋 Setup Instructions:

  1. Make sure you've deployed your site to Netlify
  2. Add TELEGRAM_BOT_TOKEN to your Netlify environment variables
  3. Click "Set Bot Webhook" below to configure the bot
  4. Test your bot by sending /start on Telegram
